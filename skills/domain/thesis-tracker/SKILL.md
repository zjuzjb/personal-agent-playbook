# Thesis Tracker

Use this skill to create or update durable thesis-tracking objects. A thesis is
not a report; it is a maintained set of claims, evidence, falsifiers, catalysts,
and watch items.

## Workflow

### Define Or Load The Thesis

Capture:

- Company and ticker.
- User reason for following or owning the name.
- Thesis statement.
- Three to five thesis pillars.
- Risks and disconfirming evidence for each pillar.
- Falsifiers: concrete facts, values, dates, or events.
- Valuation or scenario assumptions with source and formula when used.
- Current confidence and why.

### Update Log

For each evidence item:

- Date.
- Source and URL or artifact reference.
- Evidence type.
- Affected pillar or risk.
- Direction: strengthens, weakens, neutral, or unresolved.
- Confidence and why.
- Required follow-up.

### Scorecard

Track:

- Pillar.
- Original expectation.
- Current status.
- Trend.
- Evidence.
- Falsifier.

### Watch Items

Each watch item must include:

- Metric or event.
- Current value or state.
- Warning threshold.
- Positive threshold.
- Source.
- Review cadence.
- Linked pillar.
- Action if triggered.

## Quality Gates

- Every decision-relevant number has source, date, formula, period, unit, and
  provenance.
- Every pillar is falsifiable.
- New evidence maps to a pillar, risk, catalyst, valuation input, or task.
- Stale or weak evidence lowers confidence or becomes a task.
- The user's original concern remains visible.
