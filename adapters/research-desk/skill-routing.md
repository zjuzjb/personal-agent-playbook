# Research Desk Skill Routing

## Project Skills

- Next issue delivery: Research Desk issue flow adapter plus core
  `specialist-subthread` and `review-gate`.
- PR readiness: core `review-gate`.
- Post-merge cleanup: core `post-merge-cleanup`.

## Product And Research

Use `personal-equity-research-team` for:

- product direction;
- investor workflow;
- watchlist and portfolio-aware thesis tracking;
- research memo UX;
- source vault and evidence architecture;
- agent role design;
- research output shape.

Use domain specialists when needed:

- `sec-xbrl-data-engineer` for SEC/XBRL and financial data pipelines.
- `research-qa-auditor` for stale periods, provenance, thresholds, and current
  opinion gates.
- `social-research-signal-extractor` for grounded social or expert signals.
- `thesis-tracker` for falsifiable thesis objects.
- `catalyst-calendar` for watch events and follow-up loops.
- `earnings-preview` for pre-earnings setup.

## External Tools

External tools may support review, browser QA, design review, deployment, or
project management, but their internal skills are not copied into this playbook.
