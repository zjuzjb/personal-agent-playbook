# Research Desk UI Acceptance Adapter

Research Desk should feel like a concise, modern, private investment research
team workspace for individual investors.

It must not feel like:

- a macOS shell ported into the browser;
- a generic dashboard;
- a static sell-side report;
- a dense content dump;
- a demo-data prototype.

## Tab Intent

- Conclusion: current opinion, why, change conditions, and next actions.
- Business: business drivers and segment evidence that can change judgment.
- Financials: revenue, margin, cash flow, balance sheet, and earnings quality
  signals with provenance.
- Valuation: price, multiples, scenario math, thresholds, and assumptions.
- Tracking: executable watch items with current value, threshold, source,
  cadence, and action.
- Debate: strongest bull/bear evidence and what would resolve the dispute.
- Sources: provenance, freshness, missing data, and source boundaries.

## Failure Patterns

- A tab mirrors report sections instead of answering an investor concern.
- Repeated data appears without a different decision purpose.
- Evidence or provenance disappears for key numbers.
- A UI fix passes one screenshot but fails another tab or viewport.
- The page relies on demo data or untraceable runtime records.
