# Catalyst Calendar

Use this skill to track upcoming events that can change a thesis, valuation
input, risk assessment, or user watch item.

## Catalyst Object

Each catalyst should include:

- Company, ticker, or sector.
- Event name.
- Event type.
- Expected date and time.
- Source and source date.
- Confidence in timing.
- Linked thesis pillar, risk, valuation input, or research question.
- Why it matters.
- Expected evidence to collect.
- Positive threshold.
- Negative threshold.
- Follow-up action.
- Actual outcome and archive status once complete.

## Event Types

- Earnings and guidance.
- Investor day or capital markets day.
- Product launch or product milestone.
- Regulatory decision, approval, hearing, or deadline.
- Conference or trade show.
- Industry data release.
- Management or capital-allocation event.
- Relevant macro event.

## Workflow

1. Define coverage universe and time horizon.
2. Gather events from approved sources.
3. Link each event to a thesis pillar, risk, valuation input, or question.
4. Rank by decision relevance.
5. Define evidence to collect before and after the event.
6. After the event, archive outcome and update linked objects.

## Quality Gates

- Timing has source and source date.
- Impact is tied to a specific decision object.
- Thresholds are concrete where possible.
- Completed catalysts preserve actual outcome and follow-up decision.
- Recurring catalysts have cadence and next review date.
